    Questions

    How is Genesis different from a chatbot?

    A chatbot replies to messages. Genesis works toward an outcome: it plans the task, uses tools, produces files and checks the result against your goal. The conversation is how you steer it, and the finished work is what you get.

    What kind of work can it do?

    Research reports with sources, documents and briefs, slide decks, spreadsheets and financial models, data analysis, and changes to real codebases. It works with the files you give it, and every result opens next to the conversation so you can edit it directly.

    What does it remember about me?

    Only what you allow. Memory is organised by project, so a client project never leaks into a personal one. You can see every remembered item, delete any of them, or switch memory off entirely.

    Can it act without asking?

    Only within the autonomy level you choose. Reading and research can run on their own; sending email, publishing or spending money always waits for your confirmation unless you change that yourself.

    Is my data used for training?

    No. Your projects, files and conversations are not used to train models. This is the default on every plan, including the free one.

    How is my data protected?

    Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Teams get single sign-on, role-based permissions and a full audit trail of what Genesis did and who approved it.

    Is there a free trial?

    Personal is free with no time limit. Pro starts with a free first month and needs no card to begin. If Pro is not for you, you drop back to Personal and keep your projects.

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes, in two clicks from settings. Yearly plans are fully refunded within 30 days. Whatever you cancel, you can export all your projects and files first.

    Which languages does it work in?

    Genesis reads and writes in most major languages. You can mix languages inside one project, for example research sources in German and a final report in English.

    When can I start?

    Access opens in waves. Join the waitlist and we will email you when your place is ready. Waitlist members get the free Pro month automatically.
