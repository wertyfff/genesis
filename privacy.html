Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and the control you have over it, written to be read.

Policy

Concept project. Genesis is a fictional company and this text is a working template for the concept. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before any real use.

Who we are

“Genesis”, “we” and “us” refer to the company that operates this website and the Genesis products. You can reach us about privacy through the contact form.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, and the plan you selected if any.
  • Contact form: your name, email, optional company, topic and message.
  • Site preferences: your theme and cookie choice, stored in your own browser.
  • Analytics: only if you accept them, anonymous information about pages visited.

How we use it

To invite you when access opens, to answer your messages, and, with consent, to understand which pages are useful. We do not sell personal data.

Your projects and files

Content you put into Genesis products is used to do the work you ask for. It is not used to train models.

How long we keep it

Waitlist entries are kept until access opens or you ask us to remove them. Messages are kept for up to two years, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Cookies

Essential storage keeps the site working and remembers your theme and cookie choice. Analytics run only if you choose “Accept all”. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Service providers

Our website is hosted by a third-party provider that also processes form submissions on our behalf. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so on this page and update the date above.
