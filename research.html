CompanyResearchOngoing

Research for workthat gets finished.

Our research follows one question: what does a system need in order to hand over work a person can actually use?

  • Ongoing

    Goal modelling

    Turning an ambiguous request into an explicit goal, constraints and a definition of done, and asking the one question that matters when something is missing.

  • Ongoing

    Long-horizon memory

    Keeping the right context across weeks of work while staying inspectable, scoped to a project and genuinely erasable.

  • Ongoing

    Verifiable outputs

    Linking every claim to its evidence and every change to a test, so results can be checked rather than trusted.

  • Ongoing

    Calibrated autonomy

    Knowing when to act, when to ask and when to stop, and making that behaviour predictable for the people who rely on it.

  • Ongoing

    Evaluating real work

    Measuring systems on finished deliverables, such as reports, models and code changes, instead of isolated questions.

Publications

We will publish methods, evaluations and the limits we find, including the results that do not go our way. Write-ups will appear on this page as they are released.
